# Brute force enumeration — Level 1: ELI5

> **Problem:** TSP  ·  **Type:** Exact  ·  **Guarantee:** Optimal tour for very small n
> **Level 1 of 6** — explain-like-I'm-5: one analogy, no jargon. See sibling files for other levels.

---

Imagine you have to visit a few friends' houses and come back home, and you want to walk the *shortest* possible path. So you try **every single order** you could visit them in, measure how long each walk is, and pick the shortest one.

That's brute force enumeration: it checks all the possible routes and keeps the very best one. It always finds the perfect answer — but if there are lots of houses, there are *way* too many routes to try.
